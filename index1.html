Our Philosophy

NovaPeptides exists at the intersection of rigorous science and refined service. We believe that researchers deserve access to compounds of the highest possible purity — without compromise, without exception.

Quality Assurance

Every batch undergoes HPLC analysis confirming 99%+ purity, mass spectrometry for molecular identity, and endotoxin screening by ISO 17025-accredited laboratories. Full Certificates of Analysis are published for complete transparency.

Shipping and Handling

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours in temperature-controlled, light-protected packaging. Complimentary express shipping is provided on all orders above 5,000, with end-to-end tracking and cold-chain integrity.

All products are sold exclusively for in-vitro laboratory research purposes. These compounds are not approved for human or animal use and must be handled by qualified professionals in controlled environments.
