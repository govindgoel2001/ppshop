# Retatrutide vs Tirzepatide: How the Two Compounds Actually Differ

*A plain-language look at what the published research literature shows — written for research context only.*

If you follow metabolic research, you have seen these two names constantly: **tirzepatide** and **retatrutide**. They belong to the same broad class of incretin-based peptides, but they are not the same molecule, and the difference comes down to a single extra receptor.

## The core difference: dual vs triple agonism

**Tirzepatide is a dual agonist.** It activates two receptors: the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) receptor and the glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor.

**Retatrutide is a triple agonist.** It hits those same two — GIP and GLP-1 — and adds a third: the **glucagon receptor**.

That third receptor is the whole story. Glucagon-receptor agonism is associated in the literature with **increased energy expenditure** and effects on hepatic lipid handling. It is the mechanistic reason retatrutide is discussed as a potential step beyond dual agonists.

## What the published trials reported

- **Retatrutide** — In the phase 2 obesity trial (Jastreboff et al., *New England Journal of Medicine*, 2023), the highest dose produced a mean body-weight reduction of roughly **24% at 48 weeks** — among the largest reported for a single agent in this class at publication.
- **Tirzepatide** — In the pivotal **SURMOUNT-1** trial (Jastreboff et al., *NEJM*, 2022), the highest dose produced a mean reduction of about **20.9% at 72 weeks**.

One honest caveat: these were separate trials with different durations and populations. Direct head-to-head human data was still limited at the time of writing, so cross-trial comparisons should be read loosely rather than as a verdict.

## Handling and research considerations

Both are peptides supplied as lyophilised powder and reconstituted with bacteriostatic water. Both are heat-sensitive — best stored lyophilised at −20°C, with reconstituted material refrigerated at 2–8°C. Retatrutide in particular degrades when shipped or stored warm, which is why cold-chain handling and a batch-specific Certificate of Analysis matter for reproducibility.

When sourcing either compound for research, the quality checks are identical: a batch-specific **HPLC purity trace**, **mass-spectrometry** confirmation of molecular weight, and an **independent lab certificate you can verify on the lab's own website**.

## Quick answers

**Is retatrutide stronger than tirzepatide?** In published trials, retatrutide's top dose produced a larger mean weight reduction (~24% at 48 weeks) than tirzepatide's top dose in SURMOUNT-1 (~20.9% at 72 weeks) — but they were not tested head-to-head, and both are research-use-only.

**What is the main mechanistic difference?** Tirzepatide activates two receptors (GIP, GLP-1); retatrutide activates three (GIP, GLP-1, glucagon). The added glucagon activity is the defining difference.
