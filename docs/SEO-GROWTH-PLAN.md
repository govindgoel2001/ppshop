# AthenaBioLabs — SEO / AI-Search Growth Plan

_Last updated: 2026-07-08_

## The honest diagnosis

Your on-page and technical SEO is **already good** — better than most stores. Server-rendered
HTML, clean metadata, canonical tags, Organization + WebSite + Product + Offer + Breadcrumb
JSON-LD, a real sitemap, robots.txt, an `llms.txt`, and a genuinely well-written buying guide.
The homepage **is indexed** (it shows for `site:athenabiolabs.com`).

So the reason you can't find yourself past page 16 is **not a missing tag**. It is three things
no code change can shortcut:

1. **Domain age / trust.** The domain is weeks old. Google puts new sites through a
   "sandbox" — a probation period where it withholds rankings until it trusts you.
2. **YMYL + regulated niche.** Peptides = health/"Your Money or Your Life." Google applies the
   *strictest* trust bar here. Sandbox for competitive YMYL niches runs **6–12+ months**, vs.
   ~3 months for easy niches.
3. **Zero off-page authority.** supremepeptides.in outranks you because it is older and has
   **backlinks, brand searches, and reviews** — the signals Google uses to trust a store. You
   currently have ~none. This is the single biggest gap and the one that actually decides
   rankings.

### About the "I ranked #1 with Claude" YouTube videos
Most are clickbait. The legit techniques inside them are already partly done here, and none of
them bypass the three constraints above. What's *real* in them:
- **Programmatic long-tail pages** (city × product) — targets low-competition terms where the
  sandbox bites less. Real lever, but thin/spammy versions get penalized in YMYL. Must be genuinely useful.
- **Schema markup** — done (and just improved: FAQ + shipping schema added).
- **GEO / llms.txt / answer-shaped content** — to get *cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews*.
  Partly done; can go further.
- What they *don't* show: the backlinks, brand mentions, and months of waiting that did the real work.

## What actually moves the needle (in priority order)

### Tier 1 — Off-page authority (YOU must do; I can't fabricate these)
These are ~70% of the outcome. Without them, everything below is slow.
- [ ] **Get listed / mentioned** on Reddit (r/Peptides etc.), peptide forums, and Indian
      research communities where people ask "where to buy in India." Real, helpful answers that
      mention the brand. LLMs scrape these — this is how you get into AI answers.
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** (if any physical/registered presence) + India business directories
      (Justdia, IndiaMART, Sulekha) — easy citations/backlinks that build local trust.
- [ ] **Product-aggregator / marketplace listings** that link back.
- [ ] **Brand searches**: get early customers to search "AthenaBioLabs" on Google — brand-search
      volume is a powerful trust signal. Put the brand name on every invoice, WhatsApp, package.
- [ ] **A few quality backlinks** in the first 90 days provably shortens the sandbox.

### Tier 2 — Content depth & breadth (I can build these)
- [ ] **Per-product long-form**: each product page should answer the real questions researchers
      ask (dosing protocols in research literature, storage, reconstitution, comparisons).
- [ ] **City landing pages** (buy X in Mumbai/Delhi/Bangalore/…) — ONLY if each is genuinely
      differentiated (local delivery time, local relevance), not doorway spam. Start with 4–6 top cities.
- [ ] **Comparison / educational articles** (Reta vs Tirzepatide, BPC-157 vs TB-500, how to read a COA)
      — these rank for informational queries and are highly citable by AI.
- [ ] **A `/blog` or `/research`** section for freshness signals.

### Tier 3 — Technical / GEO polish (mostly done; I can finish)
- [x] FAQPage schema on product pages + buying guide (done 2026-07-08)
- [x] Offer shipping + priceValidUntil schema (done 2026-07-08)
- [ ] Stronger `llms.txt` with answer-shaped facts and comparisons
- [ ] Internal linking between related products & guides
- [ ] Ensure every deep page is actually indexed (check GSC Coverage, not just sitemap submission)
- [ ] Image alt text / filenames as keywords

## Reality on timeline
Meaningful movement on competitive terms ("buy peptides india"): **3–9 months** with consistent
Tier 1 work. Long-tail terms ("retatrutide 10mg coa india"): **weeks to a couple months**. AI
citations (Perplexity/ChatGPT): can happen **fast** if the brand gets mentioned on Reddit/forums,
because those are scraped continuously. That's your quickest win.
